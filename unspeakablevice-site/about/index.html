About

Unspeakable Vice is a volunteer queer history research project retelling San Francisco's stories block by block. Walking tours, talks, and research recover the figures, feats, and challenges of the city's queer past from the 1700s through to present day.

Questions, ideas, or corrections: shawn@unspeakablevice.tours
