A queer history research project retelling stories block by block

Because no one hands you a history book when you come out and unless you study it in college, much of queer history can be difficult to discover.

Learn more

Walking tours

A trilogy of San Francisco gayborhoods sharing lesser known figures, feats, and challenges from the 1700s through to present day.

Learn more

Partners

Local history non-profits work with us to workshop tour content, host additional programming, and market the tours to history-loving locals.

How tickets work

General admission
The standard ticket which covers a free drink at the end of your tour and helps cover costs of research, marketing, and hardship tickets for others.
Pay what you can
Cost should never keep anyone from their own history. If you're experiencing hardship, choose this at checkout and name your price. No questions asked.
Pay it forward
Add a donation that helps keep pay-what-you-can open on every tour.
